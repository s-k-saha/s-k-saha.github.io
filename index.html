About

Photo

I am a PhD student at the Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz, Germany. My work is to build microscopical models to predict meso/macro-scopic properties of physical systems. More specifically, I try to connect to bulk and interfacial properties of electrolytes near the three phase contact line of polar solvents (like water) starting from microscopic properties of the consitutents.
I am further interested in the broad field of Active Matter. Currently I am working with Active-Phase separation in model systems where we expect the Nucleation behavior to be affected by memory-induced feedback in among the active constituents.

Before joining JGU Mainz, I completed my Master's at IISER Kolkata, India with my prorject supervised by Prof. Pradeep Kumar Mohanty.

Outside the lab I am interested in the philosophy of time, dynamical systems, and the history of thermodynamics.
