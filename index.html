Research

01

Stochastic Thermodynamics

Fluctuation theorems, entropy production rates, and the thermodynamic uncertainty relations in driven colloidal and chemical systems.

02

Active Matter

Self-propelled particles, motility-induced phase separation, and collective dynamics far from thermal equilibrium.

03

Large Deviation Theory

Rare events, current fluctuations, and the geometry of the space of non-equilibrium steady states.

04

Biological Networks

Energetic costs of precision in biochemical oscillators and sensing networks, from the Jarzynski equality to living cells.
